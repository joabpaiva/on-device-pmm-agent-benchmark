# Sales Battlecard: Aurora 14 vs Vantage Pro 16

## Positioning
The Aurora 14 delivers premium performance in a portable form factor with superior AI capability, memory flexibility, and service. The Vantage Pro 16 offers more screen real estate, larger GPU memory, and a lower entry price for users prioritizing display size and raw graphics capacity.

## Aurora 14 Strengths
1. **Memory Upgradeability:** Supports up to 128 GB with 2 user-upgradeable slots, allowing buyers to start at 32 GB and grow. The Vantage Pro 16 is limited to 64 GB with soldered memory.
2. **AI Performance:** Features a 60 TOPS NPU compared to the Vantage Pro's 40 TOPS, providing enough memory headroom to run ~30B-parameter open models locally.
3. **Portability & Warranty:** Weighs 0.65 kg less than the Vantage and includes a standard 3-year on-site warranty versus a 1-year mail-in depot service.

## Genuine Vantage Pro 16 Strengths
1. **Display:** Features a larger 16-inch IPS screen with a 165 Hz refresh rate, which reviewers consistently praise for visualization work.
2. **GPU Memory:** Offers 24 GB of VRAM compared to 16 GB, providing an advantage for large 3D scenes and bigger GPU-bound models.

## Likely Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "The Vantage Pro is $300 cheaper." | The price difference is offset by the Aurora's 3-year on-site warranty (vs. 1-year mail-in), upgradeable memory that avoids replacement costs, and lighter weight that may reduce total cost of ownership through travel savings. |
| "I need 16-inch screen real estate for my visualization work." | We acknowledge the Vantage Pro's 165 Hz 16-inch display is praised for visualization. However, the Aurora 14's OLED 14" panel delivers 2880 × 1800 resolution (higher pixel density) and is 0.65 kg lighter, making it ideal for mobile workstations where screen quality and portability matter more than size. |
| "My team needs 24 GB GPU memory for large models." | For large GPU-bound models, the Vantage Pro does lead with 24 GB. However, analysts expect memory capacity to matter more as teams run larger AI models on the device, and the Aurora's 60 TOPS NPU with 128 GB upgradeable RAM offers a more flexible path for AI workloads. |

## Discovery Question
"Are you currently running local AI models or visualization workloads that require more GPU memory, and do you need that capacity to be upgradeable as your requirements grow?"
