**Q1. If I buy the base model with 32 GB of memory, can I upgrade to 128 GB myself later?**

Yes. The device supports up to 128 GB of memory and customers may upgrade memory themselves. You would use two SO-DIMM modules.
**Cited section:** Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades

**Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?**

Every Aurora 14 includes a 3-year on-site, next-business-day warranty. Customers can extend coverage to 4 or 5 years at purchase or within 90 days of purchase. Accidental damage protection is sold separately.
**Cited section:** Section 1: Warranty and Support

**Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?**

Through a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, you can run up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz. You can also add one additional display via the HDMI 2.1 port (up to 4K 120 Hz).
**Cited section:** Section 3: Docking and External Displays

**Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?**

The system's self-healing BIOS continuously checks firmware integrity. If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot.
**Cited section:** Section 4: Security Features
