# Aurora 14 AI Workstation: Customer Documentation

*Fictional product created for benchmark testing.*

## Section 1: Warranty and Support
Every Aurora 14 includes a 3-year on-site, next-business-day warranty. A technician comes to the customer's location after remote diagnosis confirms a hardware fault. Customers can extend coverage to 4 or 5 years at purchase or within 90 days of purchase. Accidental damage protection is sold separately.

## Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades
Aurora 14 has two SO-DIMM memory slots and supports up to 128 GB. Customers may upgrade memory themselves. Upgrading does not void the warranty when customers use qualified modules from the Aurora compatibility list. The device has two M.2 slots for NVMe SSDs, supporting up to 4 TB in total.

## Section 3: Docking and External Displays
Through a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, Aurora 14 supports up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz. The HDMI 2.1 port supports one additional display at up to 4K 120 Hz.

## Section 4: Security Features
Aurora 14 includes TPM 2.0, a fingerprint reader and an IR camera for face sign-in. Its self-healing BIOS continuously checks firmware integrity; if the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot. Drive encryption is supported through the operating system.

## Section 5: Power and Battery
The 86 Wh battery supports fast charging to 50% in about 30 minutes with the included 140 W adapter. Rated battery life is up to 11 hours of local video playback, measured at 150 nits with Wi-Fi off.
