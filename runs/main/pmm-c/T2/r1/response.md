BATTLECARD: Aurora 14 vs Vantage Pro 16

POSITIONING
Aurora 14 is the lighter, upgradeable choice for teams running AI on the device, and it comes with stronger service coverage. Vantage Pro 16 costs less and offers a larger screen, more GPU memory and longer rated battery life.

AURORA 14 STRENGTHS
1. Memory: Up to 128 GB, user-upgradeable. Vantage maxes out at 64 GB, soldered. Buyers can start at 32 GB and grow.
2. On-device AI: The NPU is 60 TOPS versus 40 TOPS. Memory headroom supports ~30B-parameter open models locally.
3. Mobility and service: 1.65 kg versus 2.30 kg. It has two Thunderbolt 5 ports versus one Thunderbolt 4. The standard warranty is 3-year on-site versus 1-year depot.

VANTAGE PRO 16 STRENGTHS
1. Display: The 16" screen runs at 165 Hz. Reviewers consistently praise it for visualization work.
2. GPU memory: 24 GB versus 16 GB. This helps with large 3D scenes and bigger GPU-bound models.

LIKELY OBJECTIONS
1. "Vantage is $300 cheaper."
Response: That's correct ($2,599 vs $2,899). Aurora includes a 3-year on-site warranty, and its memory can be upgraded later. Vantage's memory is soldered. Compare the cost of ownership, not only the starting price.

2. "Vantage has more GPU memory and a bigger screen."
Response: Agreed. For large 3D scenes, 24 GB is an advantage. Aurora's case is 128 GB of system memory, a faster NPU and lower weight. Two independent reviews also noted audible fan noise on the Vantage under sustained GPU load.

3. "Vantage's battery lasts longer."
Response: Its rating is 13 h versus 11 h, both measured on video playback. Aurora is 0.65 kg lighter, which matters for people who carry it daily. Suggest comparing both on the customer's own workload.

DISCOVERY QUESTION
"Over the next three years, will this machine mostly run larger AI models locally, or mostly handle large 3D scenes and visualization?"
