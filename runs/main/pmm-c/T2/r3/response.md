AURORA 14 vs VANTAGE PRO 16: SALES BATTLECARD

POSITIONING
Aurora 14 is the lighter, more expandable choice. It suits buyers who want on-device AI, memory that can grow, and 3-year on-site service. Vantage Pro 16 costs less and offers a larger, faster screen and more GPU memory, so the right pick depends on the workload.

AURORA 14 STRENGTHS
1. Memory: up to 128 GB, user-upgradeable. Buyers can start at 32 GB and grow. Vantage tops out at 64 GB, soldered.
2. On-device AI: 60 TOPS NPU (vs 40) and enough memory headroom to run ~30B-parameter open models locally.
3. Portability and service: 1.65 kg (0.65 kg lighter), 2 × Thunderbolt 5 (vs 1 × Thunderbolt 4), and a 3-year on-site warranty (vs 1-year depot).

VANTAGE PRO 16 STRENGTHS
1. Display and GPU: 16" 165 Hz screen, praised by reviewers for visualization work. Its 24 GB of GPU memory (vs 16 GB) helps with large 3D scenes.
2. Price and battery: starts $300 lower ($2,599) and is rated for 13 h of video playback (vs 11 h).

LIKELY OBJECTIONS

1. "Aurora costs $300 more."
Response: That is true. The higher price includes a 3-year on-site warranty instead of 1-year mail-in. It also includes memory that can grow to 128 GB, where Vantage's 64 GB is fixed.

2. "Vantage has more GPU memory and a bigger, faster screen."
Response: Agreed, and that matters for large 3D scenes and visualization. If the work is memory-heavy or AI-driven, Aurora's 128 GB ceiling and 60 TOPS NPU may matter more. Aurora's display is a 14" OLED at 2880 × 1800.

3. "Vantage battery lasts longer."
Response: Its rating is 2 hours longer (13 h vs 11 h, both on video playback). Aurora weighs 0.65 kg less, which helps people who carry it daily. Battery life under other workloads is not covered in the brief.
