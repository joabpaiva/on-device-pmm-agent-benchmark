Aurora 14: A 14-Inch Workstation That Runs AI Models on the Device

Your engineers and data scientists want to run AI models and professional applications without waiting on a shared server. The Aurora 14 AI Workstation, starting at $2,899 for the base configuration and available in Q4, is a 14-inch mobile workstation built to do that work on the device.

LOCAL AI AND GRAPHICS POWER

The Aurora 14 uses the Helix H9 AI processor, with 16 cores: six performance and ten efficiency. An integrated NPU is rated at 60 TOPS.

The headline capability is on-device language models. In configurations with 64 GB of memory or more, the Aurora 14 runs open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters. Memory is 32 GB as standard, so if your teams need this, specify 64 GB or more when you order.

For engineering firms, that means teams can use open-weight language models directly on their own machines. The limit is about 30 billion parameters, so check the models you plan to use against it before you standardize on a configuration.

For visual work, a Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU with 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory is included. The 14-inch OLED display runs at 2880 × 1800 and 120 Hz, with 100% DCI-P3 color gamut and 500 nits peak brightness.

UPGRADE THE MACHINE INSTEAD OF REPLACING IT

Needs differ across your teams. The Aurora 14 lets you configure for each role and adjust later.

Memory is 32 GB as standard and configurable up to 128 GB. It has two SO-DIMM slots, and memory is user-upgradeable. Storage starts at 1 TB NVMe and is configurable up to 4 TB, with two M.2 slots. Because memory and storage are configurable, one model can cover several roles. That can simplify what you stock and support.

That gives you a practical path. Buy a base configuration for roles with lighter needs. Order 64 GB or more for people who will run local models. For peripherals, the Aurora 14 has two Thunderbolt 5 ports, one USB-A 3.2 port, one HDMI 2.1 port, an SD card reader and a 3.5 mm headphone jack.

SECURITY, SUPPORT AND PORTABILITY

The Aurora 14 includes a fingerprint reader and IR camera, TPM 2.0 and a self-healing BIOS. It ships with Windows 11 Pro. Your security team can assess these features against your own policies.

Support is part of the standard package. The Aurora 14 comes with a 3-year on-site, next-business-day service warranty, extendable to 5 years. Both the 3-year term and the on-site, next-business-day service are standard. The 5-year option lets you match coverage to your refresh cycle.

Your mobile users benefit too. The aluminum chassis starts at 1.65 kg (3.64 lb). The 86 Wh battery is rated for up to 11 hours of local video playback, measured at 150 nits with Wi-Fi off. That figure is for video playback, not AI or rendering workloads.
