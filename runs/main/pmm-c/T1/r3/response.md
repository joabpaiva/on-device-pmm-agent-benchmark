Aurora 14: An AI-Ready Mobile Workstation Your IT Team Can Support

Engineering teams want to run AI models and professional applications on the laptop, not only in the data center. The Aurora 14 is a 14-inch mobile workstation built for that job, starting at $2,899 for the base configuration and arriving in Q4.

RUN AI MODELS ON THE DEVICE

The Aurora 14 pairs the Helix H9 AI processor with an integrated NPU rated at 60 TOPS. The processor has 16 cores: six performance and ten efficiency. A Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU adds 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory.

In configurations with 64 GB of memory or more, the Aurora 14 runs open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters on the device. The base configuration ships with 32 GB, so plan your memory tier around the models your engineers intend to use. For your engineers, that means the model runs on the machine in their hands, not on a remote server.

Local execution gives your team an option beside hosted services. Whether it fits your data policies is a question for your security team. Compliance certifications are not covered in the launch documentation, so confirm requirements with your sales contact.

UPGRADE INSTEAD OF REPLACE

Memory is configurable from 32 GB standard up to 128 GB, and the two SO-DIMM slots are user-upgradeable. Storage starts at 1 TB NVMe and is configurable up to 4 TB, with two M.2 slots.

For IT, that can mean a workload that outgrows its configuration does not always require a new purchase. You can also match memory and storage to each role.

Starting price is $2,899 for the base configuration with 32 GB of memory and a 1 TB SSD. Higher memory and storage tiers are configurable. Pricing for those tiers is not covered in the launch documentation, so ask us for a quote.

Service is covered too. Every Aurora 14 includes a 3-year on-site warranty with next-business-day service, extendable to 5 years. That gives you a defined service path for engineers who depend on their machines.

BUILT-IN SECURITY AND EVERYDAY PORTABILITY

The Aurora 14 includes a fingerprint reader, an IR camera, TPM 2.0 and a self-healing BIOS. It ships with Windows 11 Pro.

Weight starts at 1.65 kg (3.64 lb) in an aluminum chassis. The 86 Wh battery offers up to 11 hours of local video playback, measured at 150 nits with Wi-Fi off. That figure is for video playback. The launch documentation does not give battery life for AI or CAD workloads.

The 14-inch OLED display runs at 2880 × 1800 and 120 Hz, with 100% DCI-P3 coverage and 500 nits peak brightness. Ports include two Thunderbolt 5, one USB-A 3.2, one HDMI 2.1, an SD card reader and a 3.5 mm headphone jack.

Together, these details give IT a clear list of what ships in the box and what to verify before you buy.
