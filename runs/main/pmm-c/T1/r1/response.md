Aurora 14: A 14-Inch Workstation That Runs AI Locally and Is Built to Be Serviced

The Aurora 14 AI Workstation is a 14-inch mobile workstation for engineers, designers and data scientists who need to run AI models and professional applications on the device. It starts at $2,899 for the base configuration and is available in Q4.

1. Run AI models on the device

Your engineers want AI tools. You have to decide where those tools run. The Aurora 14 gives you the option of running them on the laptop itself.

The Helix H9 AI processor has 16 cores, six performance and ten efficiency. An integrated NPU is rated at 60 TOPS. In configurations with 64 GB of memory or more, the Aurora 14 runs open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters on the device. The base configuration ships with 32 GB, so specify 64 GB or more if local language models are the goal.

Running a model on the device means that workload does not need to go to an outside service. That gives IT another deployment option to weigh alongside cloud tools.

For graphics work, a Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU with 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory is included. The 14-inch OLED display runs at 2880 × 1800 and 120 Hz, with 500 nits peak brightness and 100% DCI-P3 color gamut.

2. Upgrade it and service it, rather than replace it

Needs change over a hardware lifecycle. The Aurora 14 gives you room to adapt. Memory is 32 GB standard and configurable up to 128 GB, in two SO-DIMM slots, and it is user-upgradeable. Storage is a 1 TB NVMe SSD as standard, configurable up to 4 TB, with two M.2 slots.

Support is covered as well. The standard warranty is three years, on-site, with next-business-day service. On-site service means support comes to your location. The warranty can be extended to five years, which lets you align coverage with your planned refresh cycle.

This post covers the base price only. Pricing for larger memory or storage configurations and for warranty extensions should be confirmed with your sales contact.

3. Built-in security and a portable design

Security features include a fingerprint reader, an IR camera, TPM 2.0 and a self-healing BIOS. These give IT built-in options for protecting the device and verifying users. The system ships with Windows 11 Pro. The spec does not detail how the self-healing BIOS works, so ask for that detail during evaluation.

The aluminum chassis weighs from 1.65 kg (3.64 lb). Two Thunderbolt 5 ports, one USB-A 3.2 port, one HDMI 2.1 port, an SD card reader and a 3.5 mm headphone jack cover common connections.

The 86 Wh battery is rated for up to 11 hours of local video playback, measured at 150 nits with Wi-Fi off. That figure describes video playback. Battery life under AI, CAD or simulation workloads is not published here, so validate it in your own pilot.

Plan your Aurora 14 evaluation

The Aurora 14 is available in Q4, starting at $2,899 for the base configuration. If your engineers need AI and professional applications on the device, start planning now. Decide which memory configuration fits your workloads, especially if local language models matter. Then contact your sales representative to discuss configurations and warranty options.
