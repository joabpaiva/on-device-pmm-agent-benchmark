Q1. If I buy the base model with 32 GB of memory, can I upgrade to 128 GB myself later?

Yes, as far as the documentation shows. Aurora 14 has two SO-DIMM memory slots and supports up to 128 GB. Customers may upgrade memory themselves. The warranty stays valid if you use qualified modules from the Aurora compatibility list.
Source: Section 2 (Memory and Storage Upgrades), with the warranty condition from Section 2.

Two points the documentation does not cover:
- It does not say what memory the base model ships with, so the 32 GB starting point is not confirmed.
- It does not say which module sizes reach 128 GB or whether the existing modules must be replaced. Check the Aurora compatibility list or ask Aurora support.

Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?

Every Aurora 14 includes a 3-year on-site, next-business-day warranty. A technician comes to your location after remote diagnosis confirms a hardware fault. You can extend coverage to 4 or 5 years, either at purchase or within 90 days of purchase. Accidental damage protection is sold separately and is not part of the standard warranty.
Source: Section 1 (Warranty and Support).

Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?

Through a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, Aurora 14 supports:
- up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or
- two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz.

Separately, the laptop's HDMI 2.1 port supports one additional display at up to 4K 120 Hz. That display connects to the laptop, not the dock.
Source: Section 3 (Docking and External Displays).

Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?

The self-healing BIOS continuously checks firmware integrity. If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot.
Source: Section 4 (Security Features).
