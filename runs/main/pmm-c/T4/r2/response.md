Q1. If I buy the base model with 32 GB of memory, can I upgrade to 128 GB myself later?

Yes, based on Section 2 (Memory and Storage Upgrades). Aurora 14 has two SO-DIMM slots and supports up to 128 GB. Customers may upgrade memory themselves. The upgrade does not void the warranty if you use qualified modules from the Aurora compatibility list.

The documentation doesn't describe the base model's memory configuration. For example, it doesn't say whether the 32 GB uses one slot or both. Before you buy modules, confirm this with Aurora sales or technical support.

Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?

This is answered in Section 1 (Warranty and Support).
- Every Aurora 14 includes a 3-year on-site, next-business-day warranty. A technician comes to your location after remote diagnosis confirms a hardware fault.
- You can extend coverage to 4 or 5 years. You can do this at purchase or within 90 days of purchase.
- Accidental damage protection is sold separately.

Extension pricing is not covered in the provided documentation. Aurora sales can confirm it.

Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?

This is answered in Section 3 (Docking and External Displays). Through a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, Aurora 14 supports either:
- up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or
- two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz.

The HDMI 2.1 port on the laptop supports one additional display at up to 4K 120 Hz. The documentation lists this port separately from the dock.

Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?

This is answered in Section 4 (Security Features). Aurora 14's self-healing BIOS continuously checks firmware integrity. If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot.
