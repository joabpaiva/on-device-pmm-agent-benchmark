# Workstation Comparison: Aurora 14, Summit X15, Vantage Pro 16

Sources: inputs/t6/aurora14.md, inputs/t6/summit_x15.txt, inputs/t6/vantage_pro16.csv

| Attribute | Aurora 14 | Summit X15 | Vantage Pro 16 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPU | Helix H9 AI, 16 cores | Helix H7 AI, 12 cores | Corvex X12, 14 cores |
| GPU | Vireo V8000 Pro, 16 GB dedicated memory | Vireo V6000, 8 GB graphics memory | Vireo V9000, 24 GB |
| Max memory | 128 GB (user-upgradeable) | 96 GB | 64 GB (soldered) |
| Max storage | 4 TB NVMe | not stated | 2 TB |
| Display | 14-inch OLED, 2880 x 1800, 120 Hz | 15.6-inch OLED, 3840 x 2400, 60 Hz | 16-inch IPS, 2560 x 1600, 165 Hz |
| Weight | 1.65 kg | 1.95 kg | 2.30 kg |
| Battery | 86 Wh | not stated | 99 Wh |
| Ports | 2 x Thunderbolt 5, 1 x USB-A, HDMI 2.1, SD card reader | 2 x Thunderbolt 4, 1 x USB-A, HDMI 2.1 | not stated |

Notes:
- The Summit X15 file says storage and battery options "will be announced closer to launch."
- The Summit X15 and Vantage Pro 16 files do not say whether memory is user-upgradeable, except that Vantage memory is soldered.
- Battery capacity (Wh) is not a battery-life figure. The files give no runtime.

## Where competitors are stronger

- Vantage Pro 16 has the largest GPU memory (24 GB vs 16 GB), the largest battery (99 Wh vs 86 Wh) and the fastest display refresh (165 Hz vs 120 Hz).
- Summit X15 has the highest display resolution (3840 x 2400) and the largest OLED panel (15.6-inch).

## Three differentiators for Aurora 14

1. Most memory, and you can upgrade it yourself.
   Aurora 14 goes up to 128 GB, user-upgradeable. Summit X15 tops out at 96 GB. Vantage Pro 16 tops out at 64 GB, soldered. That is twice the Vantage ceiling, and it is not locked at purchase.

2. The lightest of the three.
   At 1.65 kg, Aurora 14 is 0.30 kg lighter than Summit X15 (1.95 kg) and 0.65 kg lighter than Vantage Pro 16 (2.30 kg). It also has the most CPU cores in the table (16, against 12 and 14). The trade-off is a smaller 86 Wh battery than Vantage Pro 16 (99 Wh). Summit X15's battery is not stated.

3. The highest storage ceiling stated.
   Aurora 14 supports up to 4 TB NVMe, double Vantage Pro 16's 2 TB. Summit X15's storage is not stated, so no comparison is possible there. Confirm with the Summit X15 product owner before comparing against it.
