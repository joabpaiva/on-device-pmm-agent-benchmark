## Aurora 14 Differentiators

1. **Highest memory expandability**: Supports up to 128 GB and is user-upgradeable, whereas the Summit X15 caps at 96 GB and the Vantage Pro 16 is limited to 64 GB soldered memory.

2. **Lightest chassis at 1.65 kg**: Weighs less than both competitors (Summit X15: 1.95 kg; Vantage Pro 16: 2.30 kg), making it the most portable option.

3. **Fastest connectivity with Thunderbolt 5**: Equipped with two Thunderbolt 5 ports, while the Summit X15 offers only Thunderbolt 4, and the Vantage Pro 16 does not state its port configuration.
