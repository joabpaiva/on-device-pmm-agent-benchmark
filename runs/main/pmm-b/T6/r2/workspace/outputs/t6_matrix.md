## Aurora 14 Differentiators

1. **Most portable workstation**: At 1.65 kg, Aurora 14 is the lightest of the three, weighing 0.30 kg less than Summit X15 and 0.65 kg less than Vantage Pro 16.

2. **Highest memory expandability**: Aurora 14 supports up to 128 GB of user-upgradeable memory, exceeding Summit X15 (96 GB) and Vantage Pro 16 (64 GB soldered).

3. **Most versatile connectivity**: Aurora 14 includes the latest Thunderbolt 5 ports and an SD card reader, offering newer interface standards and media card support that the other models lack.
