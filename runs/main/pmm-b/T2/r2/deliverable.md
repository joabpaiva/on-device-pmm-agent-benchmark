# Sales Battlecard: Aurora 14 vs Vantage Pro 16

**Positioning Summary**
The Aurora 14 is a premium, upgradeable AI workstation that balances extreme portability with future-proof memory capacity for local model workloads. While the Vantage Pro 16 offers a lower entry price and larger screen, Aurora delivers superior long-term value through performance headroom and on-site service.

**3 Aurora Strengths**
*   **Upgradeable Memory:** Supports up to 128 GB via user-accessible slots, whereas Vantage is limited to 64 GB soldered RAM.
*   **AI Performance:** Features a 60 TOPS NPU (vs. 40 TOPS) with sufficient memory to run ~30B-parameter open models locally.
*   **Portability & Service:** Weighs 0.65 kg less than the competitor and includes a standard 3-year on-site warranty versus 1-year mail-in.

**2 Genuine Vantage Strengths**
*   **Display & GPU VRAM:** Offers a larger 16-inch screen at 165 Hz and 24 GB of GPU memory, which benefits large 3D scenes and visualization.
*   **Price & Battery:** Starts $300 lower and provides longer rated battery life (13 hours vs. 11 hours video playback).

**Likely Objections & Responses**
*   **Objection:** "Vantage is cheaper."
    *   **Response:** The $300 savings comes with soldered memory limits; Aurora's upgradeability prevents costly replacements when your team needs more RAM for larger AI models.
*   **Objection:** "We need the bigger screen for design work."
    *   **Response:** The Vantage's 16-inch panel does excel for visualization, but Aurora's 14-inch OLED offers higher resolution (2880 × 1800) in a significantly lighter chassis for mobile teams.
*   **Objection:** "Battery life is critical for our field users."
    *   **Response:** Vantage leads by two hours on video playback tests, though Aurora still delivers up to 11 hours while weighing 0.65 kg less.

**Discovery Question**
"Given that analysts expect memory capacity to matter more as teams run larger AI models on-device, what is your three-year plan for local model size and RAM requirements?"
