**Positioning Summary**
The Aurora 14 is a premium, portable workstation designed for AI professionals who need maximum memory flexibility and on-device AI performance. While the Vantage Pro 16 offers a lower entry price and larger screen, the Aurora 14 delivers superior long-term value through upgradeability and faster connectivity.

**Aurora 14 Strengths**
1. **Memory Flexibility:** Supports up to 128 GB of user-upgradeable RAM, whereas Vantage Pro 16 is limited to 64 GB soldered memory.
2. **AI Performance:** Features a 60 TOPS NPU compared to Vantage's 40 TOPS, enabling local execution of ~30B-parameter open models.
3. **Portability and Connectivity:** Weighs 0.65 kg less than the Vantage and includes two Thunderbolt 5 ports versus one Thunderbolt 4.

**Genuine Vantage Pro 16 Strengths**
1. **Display and GPU Memory:** Offers a larger 16-inch screen with 165 Hz refresh rate and 24 GB of GPU memory, beneficial for large 3D scenes.
2. **Price and Battery:** Starts at $2,599 ($300 less than Aurora) and provides up to 13 hours of video playback versus Aurora's 11 hours.

**Customer Objections and Responses**

*Objection: "The Vantage Pro 16 is $300 cheaper."*
Response: While the starting price is lower, the Aurora 14's user-upgradeable memory allows you to start with less RAM and expand later, avoiding the need to replace the entire system when your memory needs grow.

*Objection: "We need the larger screen and more GPU memory for 3D work."*
Response: The Vantage Pro 16 does excel in those specific areas with its 16-inch display and 24 GB GPU memory; however, for most AI workflows, the Aurora's faster NPU and system memory capacity provide better overall performance.

*Objection: "The battery life on Aurora is shorter."*
Response: The Aurora 14 offers up to 11 hours of video playback, which is only 2 hours less than the Vantage, while being significantly lighter and offering double the Thunderbolt connectivity for mobile work.
