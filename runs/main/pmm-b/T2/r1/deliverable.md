**Positioning Summary**
Aurora 14 is a premium, future-proof workstation built for mobile professionals who need maximum memory flexibility, leading AI performance, and superior service. Vantage Pro 16 is a cost-effective alternative for stationary users prioritizing screen real estate, GPU memory capacity, and battery life over portability.

**Aurora 14 Strengths**
1. **Memory Scalability:** Supports up to 128 GB of user-upgradeable RAM via two slots, compared to 64 GB soldered on the Vantage.
2. **AI Performance:** Features a 60 TOPS NPU and sufficient memory headroom to run ~30B-parameter open models locally, outperforming the Vantage's 40 TOPS NPU.
3. **Portability and Connectivity:** Weighs 0.65 kg less (1.65 kg total) and includes two Thunderbolt 5 ports versus the Vantage's single Thunderbolt 4 port.

**Genuine Vantage Pro 16 Strengths**
1. **Display and GPU Memory:** Offers a larger 16-inch screen with 165 Hz refresh rate praised for visualization work, plus 24 GB of GPU memory beneficial for large 3D scenes.
2. **Battery and Price:** Provides longer rated battery life (13 hours vs. 11 hours) and a lower starting price ($2,599 vs. $2,899).

**Customer Objections and Responses**
*Objection: "The Aurora costs $300 more."*
Response: The Aurora includes a 3-year on-site warranty versus the Vantage's 1-year depot service, and its user-upgradeable memory allows you to start with less RAM and add capacity later, protecting your investment.

*Objection: "We need the Vantage's 24 GB GPU for our 3D workloads."*
Response: For GPU-bound tasks involving large 3D scenes, the Vantage's 24 GB is an advantage; however, if your workflow involves running local AI models, the Aurora's 60 TOPS NPU and 128 GB system memory provide critical headroom the Vantage cannot match.

*Objection: "The Aurora battery life is shorter."*
Response: The Aurora delivers up to 11 hours of video playback in a chassis that is 0.65 kg lighter, making it significantly easier to carry all day while still meeting typical mobile work requirements.
