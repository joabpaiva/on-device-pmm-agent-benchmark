Bring AI to the Edge: The Aurora 14 Workstation for Engineering Teams

The Aurora 14 delivers on-device AI performance in a 14-inch mobile workstation built for engineers and designers. This system runs open-weight language models locally while maintaining the portability and serviceability that mid-size engineering firms require.

On-Device AI That Accelerates Development Workflows

Your engineering teams spend significant time waiting for cloud-based AI tools to process data and generate results. The Aurora 14 changes this equation with a Helix H9 AI processor featuring an integrated NPU rated at 60 TOPS. This capability enables the system to run open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters directly on the device in configurations with 64 GB of memory or more.

For engineering applications, this means faster iteration cycles. Designers can query technical documentation, generate code snippets, or analyze simulation results without network latency. Data scientists can prototype models locally before deploying to production environments. The 16-core processor architecture—combining 6 performance cores with 10 efficiency cores—maintains responsiveness during these intensive workloads.

The Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU with 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory complements the NPU for graphics-intensive tasks. Whether rendering CAD assemblies, training computer vision models, or accelerating machine learning inference, the system provides the compute headroom that professional applications demand.

Upgradeability That Protects Your Investment

IT teams managing workstation fleets understand that technology requirements evolve faster than refresh cycles. The Aurora 14 addresses this reality with user-upgradeable memory and storage. Two SO-DIMM slots allow configurations from 32 GB standard up to 128 GB, letting you start with base specifications and add capacity as project demands grow.

Similarly, two M.2 slots support storage expansion from 1 TB NVMe SSD standard up to 4 TB. This flexibility means you can deploy base configurations to new hires and upgrade specific machines when large datasets or complex simulations require additional capacity. Unlike sealed workstations that require complete replacement, the Aurora 14 adapts to changing needs without forcing premature refreshes.

The 3-year on-site, next-business-day service warranty standard reduces downtime when issues arise. Extendable to 5 years, this coverage provides predictable service costs and minimizes disruption to engineering schedules.

Portability Without Compromising Professional Connectivity

Mobile workstations must balance performance with practical deployment. The Aurora 14 weighs starting at 1.65 kg (3.64 lb) in an aluminum chassis, making it viable for engineers who move between design reviews, client sites, and collaborative workspaces.

The 14-inch OLED display delivers 2880 × 1800 resolution at 120 Hz with 500 nits peak brightness and 100% DCI-P3 color gamut. This ensures accurate color representation for design work while maintaining readability in various lighting conditions.

Connectivity supports professional workflows without dongles. Two Thunderbolt 5 ports handle high-speed data transfer and external GPU configurations. One USB-A 3.2 port maintains compatibility with legacy peripherals. HDMI 2.1 connects to external displays and presentation systems. An SD card reader serves photographers and video professionals. The 3.5 mm headphone jack supports audio peripherals for collaboration tools.

Battery capacity reaches up to 11 hours of local video playback measured at 150 nits with Wi-Fi off. Security features including fingerprint reader, IR camera, TPM 2.0, and self-healing BIOS protect sensitive engineering data. The system ships with Windows 11 Pro for enterprise management compatibility.

Contact your sales representative to discuss Aurora 14 deployment options for your engineering team. Base configurations start at $2,899, with availability in Q4.
