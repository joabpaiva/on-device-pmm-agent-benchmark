# Aurora 14 AI Workstation: Product Specification Sheet

*Fictional product created for benchmark testing. Not a real device.*

**Positioning:** A 14-inch mobile workstation built for engineers, designers and data scientists who need to run AI models and professional applications on the device.

**Starting price:** $2,899 (base configuration)
**Availability:** Q4

## Processor and AI
- Helix H9 AI processor, 16 cores (6 performance, 10 efficiency)
- Integrated NPU rated at 60 TOPS
- Runs open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters on the device in configurations with 64 GB of memory or more

## Graphics
- Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU with 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory

## Memory
- 32 GB standard, configurable up to 128 GB
- Two SO-DIMM slots; memory is user-upgradeable

## Storage
- 1 TB NVMe SSD standard, configurable up to 4 TB
- Two M.2 slots

## Display
- 14-inch OLED, 2880 × 1800, 120 Hz
- 500 nits peak brightness, 100% DCI-P3 color gamut

## Design
- Weight: starting at 1.65 kg (3.64 lb)
- Aluminum chassis

## Battery
- 86 Wh battery
- Up to 11 hours of local video playback (measured at 150 nits, Wi-Fi off)

## Ports
- 2 × Thunderbolt 5
- 1 × USB-A 3.2
- 1 × HDMI 2.1
- SD card reader
- 3.5 mm headphone jack

## Security
- Fingerprint reader and IR camera
- TPM 2.0
- Self-healing BIOS

## Software
- Ships with Windows 11 Pro

## Warranty
- 3-year on-site, next-business-day service standard
- Extendable to 5 years
