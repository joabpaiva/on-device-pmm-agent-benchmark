Q1. Yes, you can upgrade memory yourself. The documentation states: "Customers may upgrade memory themselves. Upgrading does not void the warranty when customers use qualified modules from the Aurora compatibility list." (Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades). The device supports up to 128 GB with two SO-DIMM slots.

Q2. The standard warranty is a "3-year on-site, next-business-day warranty." (Section 1: Warranty and Support). Yes, you can extend it: "Customers can extend coverage to 4 or 5 years at purchase or within 90 days of purchase."

Q3. From one Thunderbolt 5 dock, you can run up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz. The documentation also notes: "The HDMI 2.1 port supports one additional display at up to 4K 120 Hz." (Section 3: Docking and External Displays). This totals up to three external displays over one dock.

Q4. "If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot." (Section 4: Security Features).

Q5. Not covered in the provided documentation. The documentation does not list supported operating systems or mention Ubuntu 24.04 specifically. The customer should contact Aurora 14 support or check the official website for OS certification details.
