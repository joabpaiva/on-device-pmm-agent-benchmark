**Q1. If I buy the base model with 32 GB of memory, can I upgrade to 128 GB myself later?**

**Answer:** Yes. Aurora 14 has two SO-DIMM memory slots and supports up to 128 GB. Customers may upgrade memory themselves.

*Cite:* Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades

***

**Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?**

**Answer:** Aurora 14 includes a 3-year on-site, next-business-day warranty as standard. Customers can extend coverage to 4 or 5 years at purchase or within 90 days of purchase.

*Cite:* Section 1: Warranty and Support

***

**Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?**

**Answer:** Through a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, Aurora 14 supports up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz. Additionally, the HDMI 2.1 port supports one additional display at up to 4K 120 Hz.

*Cite:* Section 3: Docking and External Displays

***

**Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?**

**Answer:** Aurora 14's self-healing BIOS continuously checks firmware integrity. If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot.

*Cite:* Section 4: Security Features

***
