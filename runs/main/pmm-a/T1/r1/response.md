# Think Like a Workstation in a Screen the Size of a Notebook: Introducing the Aurora 14 AI Workstation

Engineers who lead teams across multiple sites understand that your most valuable asset is the bandwidth to collaborate without carrying redundant laptops. The Aurora 14 AI Workstation delivers workstation-grade compute in a 14-inch form factor, giving you the flexibility to work in compact meeting rooms or from any workstation setup while maintaining the performance your team relies on.

## Professional Performance Without the Physical Footprint

Mid-size engineering firms often struggle with the balance between processor power and portability. The Aurora 14 addresses this by featuring the Helix H9 AI processor with 16 cores. This allows you to run professional applications efficiently while maintaining a starting weight of 1.65 kg. The integrated NPU is rated at 60 TOPS, which enables local handling of open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters in configurations with 64 GB of memory or more. This capability allows engineers to test and prototype AI workflows that typically require the processing power of larger, more immobile machines, yet it fits easily into tight luggage overhead compartments.

## Graphics and Display Precision for Detail-Oriented Work

Design and engineering tasks often require high-fidelity visual processing. The Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU provides 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory, ensuring sufficient resources for rendering complex simulations and professional creative applications. The display panel further supports this precision with a 14-inch OLED screen offering 2880 × 1800 resolution at 120 Hz. At 500 nits peak brightness and 100% DCI-P3 color gamut, engineers can review color-critical designs with industry-standard accuracy even in large, sunlit meeting spaces.

## Future-Proof Configurations Designed for Long-Term Reliability

Your IT infrastructure investments must stand the test of time. The Aurora 14 offers flexibility in its 128 GB maximum memory capacity across two upgradeable SO-DIMM slots. Storage is configurable up to 4 TB with two M.2 slots, giving you room to expand for large project files. The system includes an 86 Wh battery, which the manufacturer rates up to 11 hours of local video playback in standard measurement conditions. Security is built into the chassis with a fingerprint reader, IR camera, and TPM 2.0. You also receive a 3-year on-site, next-business-day service standard, extendable to 5 years from the factory, ensuring minimal downtime for critical computing assets throughout their lifespan.

For IT decision-makers evaluating mobile infrastructure, the Aurora 14 represents a balanced approach to mobile performance and long-term reliability. Starting at $2,899, the device positions itself as a viable option for teams bringing professional-grade computing power into the field.
