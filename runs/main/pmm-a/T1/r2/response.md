# Meet Aurora 14: The Mobile Workstation That Fits AI Into Your Workflow

IT leaders at mid-size engineering firms know the trade-off: powerful enough to run local AI models and professional applications, or portable enough to bring to the client site. The Aurora 14 bridges that trade-off with a 14-inch design built to run open-weight language models right on the device, without sacrificing the performance required by your engineers.

## Local AI Without Compromising Portability

Engineers don’t need to choose between model complexity and mobility. The Aurora 14 runs open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters on the device in configurations with 64 GB of memory or more. This capability is paired with a Helix H9 AI processor, 16 cores (6 performance, 10 efficiency) and an integrated NPU rated at 60 TOPS to handle the load. At 1.65 kg (3.64 lb), IT can deploy the device to field engineers who still require large in-context windows without needing to connect back to a data center for inference.

## Memory and Storage That Scale With Your Team

Your engineers may start with base specs and grow into heavier workloads over time. The Aurora 14 starts with 32 GB of memory, configurable up to 128 GB with two user-upgradeable SO-DIMM slots. Storage begins at 1 TB NVMe SSD, configurable up to 4 TB across two M.2 slots. This growth path means you invest once without needing to refresh devices when your models outgrow their allocation.

## A Secure, Service-Ready Implementation

IT departments need to balance mobility with accountability, uptime, and security. The Aurora 14 comes with a fingerprint reader, IR camera, and TPM 2.0 to enforce identity and device integrity. Self-healing BIOS reduces downtime from firmware corruption events. Support is backed by a 3-year on-site, next-business-day service standard, extendable to 5 years, ensuring minimal disruption to your engineers’ schedules.

A 14-inch form factor with an aluminum chassis and 86 Wh battery keeps interim engineering work flowing on the go. Local video playback under measured conditions runs up to 11 hours at 150 nits with Wi-Fi off. The display supports 2880 × 1800 resolution at 120 Hz, with 500 nits peak brightness and 100% DCI-P3 color gamut for accurate designs and data visualization. Connectivity is handled by 2 × Thunderbolt 5, 1 × USB-A 3.2, 1 × HDMI 2.1, an SD card reader, and a 3.5 mm headphone jack.

Windows 11 Pro ships with the device out of the box for seamless integration with existing management and security infrastructure.
