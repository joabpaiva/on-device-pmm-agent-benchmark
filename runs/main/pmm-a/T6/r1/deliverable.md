2. **Best storage capacity at portable weight** - At 1.65 kg, the Aurora 14 is the lightest of the three and offers the highest storage ceiling at 4 TB NVMe, beating the Summit X15's unspecified storage and the Vantage Pro 16's 2 TB, all while being lighter than both competitors (180 g lighter than the Summit, 650 g lighter than the Vantage Pro).

3. **Superior port selection with Thunderbolt 5** - The Aurora 14 supports Thunderbolt 5 and includes an SD card reader, which neither competitor offers. While the Summit X15 uses Thunderbolt 4 and lacks an SD reader, the Aurora 14 stays at 1.65 kg despite this expanded connectivity option.
