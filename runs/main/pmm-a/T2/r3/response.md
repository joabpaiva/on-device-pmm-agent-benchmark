# Sales Battlecard: Aurora 14 vs. Vantage Pro 16

## Positioning
For mobile AI professionals who demand local compute, the Aurora 14 out-executes the Vantage Pro 16 with a more powerful NPU and upgradeable RAM. For visualization-heavy workstations prioritizing screen size and raw GPU memory, the Vantage Pro 16 remains a viable budget option.

## Aurora 14 Strengths
1. **Mobile AI Capability:** With 60 TOPS and high memory headroom, Aurora supports ~30B-parameter local models; Vantage’s 40 TOPS cannot.
2. **Hardware Future-Proofing:** 128 GB DDR5 with two upgradeable slots lets buyers start low and scale; Vantage is capped at 64 GB soldered.
3. **Connectivity & Warranty:** Dual Thunderbolt 5 ports and a standard 3-year on-site warranty simplify peripheral setups and reduce downtime.

## Vantage Pro 16 Strengths
1. **Display:** 16-inch IPS at 165 Hz is widely praised for visualization work; Aurora’s 14-inch OLED is 120 Hz.
2. **Price:** Starts at $2,599 (Vantage), $300 less than Aurora.

## Objections & Responses

| Customer Concern | Counter |
|---|---|
| $300 price difference is too high. | Aurora’s 60 TOPS NPU and upgradeable memory avoid locked-in costs; you can actually extend the device’s useful life. |
| I need long battery life. | Both run ~11–13 hours; difference is 2 hours, yet Aurora saves 650 g in weight alone. |
| Vantage has bigger GPU VRAM. | 24 GB helps 3D scenes, but Aurora’s superior NPU handles AI inference, which is trending faster than raw render workloads. |
