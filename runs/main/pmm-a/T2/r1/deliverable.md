## Sales Battlecard: Aurora 14 vs Vantage Pro 16

**Positioning**
Aurora 14 is the portable workstation for owners who need AI compute, memory headroom, and portability. Vantage Pro 16 is the desktop-replacement for buyers who want a bigger screen, more GPU memory, and a longer battery.

**Aurora 14 Strengths**
1. **Memory & Upgradeability:** 128 GB maximum (vs. 64 GB soldered). Users can start at 32 GB and upgrade. Analysts expect memory to matter more for large AI models.
2. **Local AI:** 60 TOPS NPU (vs. 40 TOPS) with sufficient memory headroom to run ~30B-parameter open models locally.
3. **Portability:** 1.65 kg vs. 2.30 kg. Easier to carry to meetings or other locations.

**Vantage Pro 16 Genuine Strengths**
1. **Screen:** 16-inch IPS, 165 Hz. Reviewers praise it for visualization work.
2. **GPU Memory:** 24 GB (vs. 16 GB). Advantage for large 3D scenes and bigger GPU-bound models.

**Likely Objections (3 each) & Responses**

*   **Online:** "The Vantage is $300 cheaper."
    *   **Response:** "That's a real benefit. However, the Aurora includes a 3-year on-site warranty versus the 1-year depot service that comes with the Vantage—that's 30 months more supported. You save $300 up front but get significantly more peace of mind and service coverage over time."

*   **Online:** "I need more screen real estate."
    *   **Response:** "We do acknowledge the Vantage has a larger 16-inch display. If you are a professional visualization user who prioritizes screen size above everything else, we'd recommend the Vantage. However, for most owners, the Aurora's OLED panel offers superior contrast and color accuracy."

*   **Online:** "I'm on a tight budget."
    *   **Response:** "We understand price is a factor. The Vantage is $300 less at launch, but remember that the Aurora comes with 3 years of on-site service vs. 1 year depot service on the Vantage. Depending on your eligibility for volume discounts or rebates, that service difference may offset the upfront cost."

**Discovery Question for the Salesperson to Ask**
1. "What portion of your workflow is mobile versus stationary? Are you spending more time moving between rooms or at a desk?"

*If they say "Mobile": Push Aurora (lighter weight, more ports, memory upgrades).*
*If they say "Stationary": Push Vantage (bigger screen, more GPU memory, more battery).*
