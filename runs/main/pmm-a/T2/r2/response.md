## Aurora 14 vs Vantage Pro 16: Sales Battlecard

### Positioning
The Aurora 14 delivers higher memory capacity, AI power, portability, and superior service. The Vantage Pro 16 offers a larger display and more GPU VRAM for a lower starting price.

### Aurora Strengths
1. **Memory upgradeability**: Supports up to 128 GB and can be expanded by the user (two slots). The Vantage Pro has 64 GB soldered. As teams run larger AI models, memory capacity will matter more.
2. **AI NPU performance**: 60 TOPS versus 40 TOPS, with the headroom to run ~30B-parameter models locally.
3. **3-year on-site warranty**: Standard service coverage versus the Vantage 1-year mail-in warranty.

### Vantage Strengths
1. Sixteen-inch IPS display at 165 Hz, which reviewers consistently praise for visualization.
2. Twenty-four GB of VRAM, an advantage for large 3D scenes and bigger GPU-bound models.

### Objections & Responses

**Objection 1**: "Why pay $300 more for less screen?"
**Response**: Agree the Vantage screen is larger. However, Aurora’s AI NPU is 50% more powerful, memory is double and upgradeable, and warranty covers three years of on-site service. Customers who run AI locally or need growth headroom see the long-term value.

**Objection 2**: "I need 24 GB of VRAM and don’t want to upgrade."
**Response**: Warranted for the Vantage 24 GB, but Aurora’s 60 TOPS NPU will perform better on AI locally—with room to spare—and towards AI-accelerated application loads.

**Objection 3**: "Why only one Thunderbolt port?"
**Response**: One Thunderbolt 4 is not enough for dual docks or hubs. Two Thunderbolt 5 ports on the Aurora support higher external bandwidth and easier setup than a single port.
