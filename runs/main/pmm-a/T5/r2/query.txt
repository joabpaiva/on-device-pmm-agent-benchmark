# Buyer Persona: IT Director, Mid-Size Engineering Firm

*Fictional persona created for benchmark testing.*

**Name:** Morgan Lee
**Role:** IT Director
**Company:** A 400-person civil engineering firm with three offices

## Situation
- The IT team is cloud-first. Engineers use cloud-hosted virtual workstations for CAD and simulation work.
- The hardware and software budget cycle closes at the end of Q1. Anything not approved by then waits a year.
- Engineers increasingly want to use AI tools on client drawings and project files.

## What Morgan cares about
- Total cost across the fleet, including the monthly cloud workstation bill.
- Security and keeping client project data under the firm's control.
- Fleet manageability: fewer device types, easy upgrades, predictable service.
- Engineer productivity on site visits, where connectivity is unreliable.

## Likely objections
- "We already pay for cloud workstations. Why buy powerful laptops too?"
- "Laptops are harder to manage and secure than cloud desktops."
- "Our budget is locked until the end of Q1."

## Preferred communication
- Short, direct emails. No hype. Facts over adjectives.

# Aurora 14 AI Workstation: Product Specification Sheet

*Fictional product created for benchmark testing. Not a real device.*

**Positioning:** A 14-inch mobile workstation built for engineers, designers and data scientists who need to run AI models and professional applications on the device.

**Starting price:** $2,899 (base configuration)
**Availability:** Q4

## Processor and AI
- Helix H9 AI processor, 16 cores (6 performance, 10 efficiency)
- Integrated NPU rated at 60 TOPS
- Runs open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters on the device in configurations with 64 GB of memory or more

## Graphics
- Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU with 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory

## Memory
- 32 GB standard, configurable up to 128 GB
- Two SO-DIMM slots; memory is user-upgradeable

## Storage
- 1 TB NVMe SSD standard, configurable up to 4 TB
- Two M.2 slots

## Display
- 14-inch OLED, 2880 × 1800, 120 Hz
- 500 nits peak brightness, 100% DCI-P3 color gamut

## Design
- Weight: starting at 1.65 kg (3.64 lb)
- Aluminum chassis

## Battery
- 86 Wh battery
- Up to 11 hours of local video playback (measured at 150 nits, Wi-Fi off)

## Ports
- 2 × Thunderbolt 5
- 1 × USB-A 3.2
- 1 × HDMI 2.1
- SD card reader
- 3.5 mm headphone jack

## Security
- Fingerprint reader and IR camera
- TPM 2.0
- Self-healing BIOS

## Software
- Ships with Windows 11 Pro

## Warranty
- 3-year on-site, next-business-day service standard
- Extendable to 5 years
