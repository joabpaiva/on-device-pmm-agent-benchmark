# Run Enterprise AI Models on the World’s Lightest 14-Inch Workstation

In today’s hybrid engineering workflow, the ability to prototype locally without sacrificing mobility or security is no longer a luxury—it’s a prerequisite. The Aurora 14 AI Workstation delivers that balance, combining workstation-grade performance with a chassis that fits easily into a backpack.

### Local AI Development Without Compromising Portability
The Aurora 14 empowers your engineering teams to train, fine-tune, and test open-weight language models directly on the device. By leveraging the Helix H9 AI processor with its 16 cores and integrated 60 TOPS NPU, the workstation can run models up to 30 billion parameters locally when paired with 64 GB of memory or more. This capability reduces reliance on cloud infrastructure for iterative development, accelerates prototyping cycles, and keeps sensitive proprietary data on-premises. The 14-inch form factor, starting at 1.65 kg (3.64 lb), ensures engineers can carry their development environment between offices, client sites, and co-working spaces without the bulk traditionally associated with AI-capable machines.

### Professional-Grade Graphics and Display for Complex Modeling
Engineering applications demand both raw computational power and precise visual fidelity. The Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU, equipped with 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory, handles complex rendering and simulation tasks with ease. Complementing this is a 14-inch OLED display offering 2,880 × 1,800 resolution at 120 Hz, with 500 nits of peak brightness and 100% DCI-P3 color gamut coverage. This combination ensures that detailed schematics, CAD models, and data visualizations remain crisp and color-accurate, even in brightly lit environments.

### Enterprise Security and Reliability for Mission-Critical Work
For mid-size engineering firms, downtime and security breaches carry significant operational and financial risks. The Aurora 14 addresses these concerns with a comprehensive security architecture that includes a fingerprint reader, IR camera, and TPM 2.0 chip, all backed by a self-healing BIOS to protect against low-level corruption. Reliability is further reinforced by an 86 Wh battery capable of up to 11 hours of local video playback and a 3-year on-site warranty with next-business-day service—a standard that can be extended to five years. The workstation ships with Windows 11 Pro, providing the enterprise-grade management and stability required for large-scale deployments.

### Upgradeable Performance for Long-Term Investment
Technology evolves rapidly, but your hardware investment should last. The Aurora 14 offers two SO-DIMM slots supporting configurations from 32 GB up to 128 GB of user-upgradeable memory, and two M.2 slots allowing storage to expand from 1 TB up to 4 TB. This flexibility ensures the workstation can grow alongside your team’s increasing computational demands without requiring a premature replacement.
