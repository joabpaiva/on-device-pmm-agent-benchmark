# Bring Your AI Workflows On The Road With The Aurora 14

Engineers and designers are no longer bound by the limitations of a desktop when their work demands high-performance computing. The Aurora 14 AI Workstation delivers the power of a mobile workstation in a compact 14-inch form factor, enabling teams to run complex AI models and professional applications anywhere.

### Run AI Models Directly On The Device

The Aurora 14 is designed to handle generative AI workloads without relying on cloud connections. At its heart is the Helix H9 AI processor, which features 16 cores—six performance cores and ten efficiency cores—and includes an integrated NPU rated at 60 TOPS. This combination allows the device to run open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters directly on the machine. This capability is particularly valuable for data scientists who need to iterate quickly on models without the latency or security concerns of off-device processing. The device achieves this local inference capability in configurations with 64 GB of memory or more, ensuring that even heavier workloads can be executed with sufficient headroom.

### Professional Performance With Scalable Memory

Professional applications require substantial memory to function efficiently, and the Aurora 14 provides a flexible solution. The system ships with 32 GB of standard memory, which is already robust for most professional workflows. However, the real advantage lies in its scalability: memory is user-upgradeable via two SO-DIMM slots and can be configured up to 128 GB. This configurability allows IT decision-makers to balance initial acquisition costs with future expansion needs. For engineers working with large datasets or complex simulations, the ability to upgrade to 128 GB ensures the device remains capable as project requirements grow.

The graphics performance complements the processor capabilities with a discrete Vireo V8000 Pro GPU featuring 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory. This combination of a powerful NPU and dedicated GPU ensures that the device can handle both AI inference and graphics-intensive rendering tasks simultaneously.

### Mobile Capabilities That Support Field Work

The Aurora 14 maintains professional-grade performance while prioritizing portability. Weighing in at starting 1.65 kg (3.64 lb) with an aluminum chassis, the device is designed for engineers who move between offices, client sites, and remote locations. The 86 Wh battery delivers up to 11 hours of local video playback, providing all-day runtime for users working away from power outlets.

The 14-inch OLED display offers a 2880 × 1800 resolution at 120 Hz with 500 nits peak brightness and 100% DCI-P3 color gamut coverage. This display quality is essential for designers reviewing color-critical work and engineers examining detailed schematics. Connectivity is handled by two Thunderbolt 5 ports, a USB-A 3.2 port, an HDMI 2.1 port, an SD card reader, and a 3.5 mm headphone jack, ensuring compatibility with existing peripherals and field equipment.

Security is built into the device with a fingerprint reader, IR camera, TPM 2.0, and self-healing BIOS, addressing the concerns of IT departments managing remote workforces.

### Deploy With Enterprise Support

The Aurora 14 ships with Windows 11 Pro and includes a 3-year on-site warranty with next-business-day service as the standard. This warranty can be extended to five years, providing organizations with long-term peace of mind and reducing downtime costs.

The device is available starting Q4 with a starting price of $2,899 for the base configuration.
