# The Aurora 14: Your AI-Ready Workstation in a 14-inch Chassis

For mid-size engineering firms, the pressure is on. You need to deliver complex simulations and AI-driven designs without the overhead of massive data center infrastructure. The Aurora 14 arrives in Q4 to solve that exact problem: a 14-inch mobile workstation that delivers the power of a desktop in a chassis you can actually carry into client meetings.

It is not about chasing every trend; it is about giving your engineering teams the tools they need to work faster, more securely, and more efficiently. The Aurora 14 is designed for the professionals who need to run heavy workloads without compromising on mobility or design integrity.

### AI Processing at Your Fingertips

The Helix H9 AI processor is the heart of this machine. With 16 cores split between performance and efficiency, it handles demanding engineering tasks without draining the battery. The integrated NPU, rated at 60 TOPS, brings artificial intelligence directly to the device. Your team can run open-weight language models up to about 30 billion parameters locally, provided the system has 64 GB of memory or more. This means sensitive data stays on the device, and your engineers do not need to rely on cloud connections for local inference tasks.

### Graphics That Get the Job Done

Professional applications demand professional-grade graphics. The Vireo V8000 Pro discrete GPU provides the raw horsepower needed for rendering complex simulations and 3D models. With 16 GB of dedicated graphics memory, the system can handle large datasets and high-resolution textures without stuttering. For teams running CAD software, simulation tools, or video editing applications, this ensures that the hardware does not become a bottleneck in your workflow.

### Mobility Without Compromise

The Aurora 14 maintains a professional footprint at 1.65 kg (3.64 lb). It features a 14-inch OLED display with 2880 × 1800 resolution and 120 Hz refresh rates for smooth scrolling through dense data. The screen reaches 500 nits peak brightness and covers 100% of the DCI-P3 color gamut, ensuring accurate color representation for design teams. The 86 Wh battery supports up to 11 hours of local video playback. The device connects to the outside world through two Thunderbolt 5 ports, a USB-A 3.2 port, an HDMI 2.1 output, and an SD card reader.

Memory and storage are user-upgradeable. The system ships with 32 GB of memory in two SO-DIMM slots, expandable up to 128 GB. Storage begins at 1 TB of NVMe SSD space and can be configured up to 4 TB across two M.2 slots. This flexibility ensures that the hardware can grow alongside your firm's expanding projects.

### Security and Reliability

In an engineering environment, data integrity and security are paramount. The device includes a fingerprint reader, an IR camera, and TPM 2.0 for hardware-based security. The self-healing BIOS adds another layer of protection against firmware corruption. The 3-year on-site warranty with next-business-day service standard reduces downtime. This warranty can be extended to 5 years for long-term peace of mind.

The system ships with Windows 11 Pro, providing a familiar and robust operating environment. Starting at $2,899 for the base configuration, the Aurora 14 offers a competitive entry point for high-performance workstations.
