**Q1. Can I upgrade from 32 GB to 128 GB myself?**
Yes. You can upgrade the memory yourself. The documentation states that the device supports up to 128 GB and customers may upgrade memory themselves.
*Source: Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades*

**Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?**
The standard warranty is 3-year on-site, next-business-day. You can extend coverage to 4 or 5 years at purchase or within 90 days of purchase.
*Source: Section 1: Warranty and Support*

**Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?**
Up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz can be run from a single Thunderbolt 5 dock. Alternatively, two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz are supported.
*Source: Section 3: Docking and External Displays*

**Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?**
The system automatically restores a protected copy of the BIOS at the next boot. The self-healing BIOS continuously checks firmware integrity and restores it if corrupted or attacked.
*Source: Section 4: Security Features*
