Here are the answers to your questions based on the Aurora 14 documentation:

**Q1. If I buy the base model with 32 GB of memory, can I upgrade to 128 GB myself later?**
**Answer:** Yes, you can upgrade the memory yourself. The device has two SO-DIMM slots that support up to 128 GB total. The documentation states that upgrading does not void the warranty when using qualified modules from the Aurora compatibility list.
**Source:** Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades

**Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?**
**Answer:** The standard warranty is a 3-year on-site, next-business-day warranty. You can extend the coverage to 4 or 5 years at the time of purchase or within 90 days of purchase.
**Source:** Section 1: Warranty and Support

**Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?**
**Answer:** Through a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, you can run up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz. If you add the HDMI 2.1 port, you can run one additional display at up to 4K 120 Hz.
**Source:** Section 3: Docking and External Displays

**Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?**
**Answer:** The system's self-healing BIOS continuously checks firmware integrity. If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot.
**Source:** Section 4: Security Features

**Q5. Is Aurora 14 certified for Ubuntu 24.04?**
**Answer:** Not covered in the provided documentation. The documentation mentions that drive encryption is supported through the operating system, but it does not list specific supported operating systems or certifications. The customer should contact the manufacturer's support team or visit their official website for compatibility information.
