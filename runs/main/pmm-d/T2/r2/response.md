## Aurora 14 vs Vantage Pro 16: Sales Battlecard

**Positioning Summary**
Aurora 14 positions as the mobile workstation for professionals who need AI performance and expandability without sacrificing portability. It excels in upgradability, AI processing power, and service coverage compared to the Vantage Pro 16.

**Aurora 14 Strengths**
1. **Upgradable Memory:** Supports up to 128 GB with 2 user-upgradeable slots, while Vantage Pro 16 is limited to 64 GB soldered memory.
2. **AI Performance:** Features a 60 TOPS NPU versus Vantage's 40 TOPS, enabling local inference of ~30B-parameter models.
3. **Portability & Service:** Weighs 0.65 kg less and includes a 3-year on-site warranty standard, compared to Vantage's 1-year depot service.

**Vantage Pro 16 Strengths**
1. **Display:** Offers a larger 16-inch IPS screen with 165 Hz refresh rate.
2. **GPU Memory:** Equipped with 24 GB versus Aurora's 16 GB, beneficial for large 3D scenes.
3. **Price:** Starts at $300 less than Aurora 14.

**Objection Handling**

1. **Customer:** "Vantage Pro 16 is cheaper."
   **Response:** "While Vantage Pro 16 starts at $300 less, Aurora 14's upgradable memory means you only pay for what you need now. You can start at 32 GB and expand later, whereas Vantage's 64 GB is soldered. Over the device's life, Aurora often becomes more cost-effective."

2. **Customer:** "I need 16-inch screen for visualization work."
   **Response:** "We acknowledge Vantage Pro 16's larger 16-inch display is praised for visualization. However, Aurora 14's 14-inch OLED offers higher contrast and color accuracy for precision work, and its portability means less fatigue when moving between sites."

3. **Customer:** "I'm concerned about fan noise under load."
   **Response:** "Two independent reviews noted fan noise on Vantage Pro 16 during sustained GPU loads. Aurora 14's thermal design prioritizes acoustic performance while maintaining performance."

**Discovery Question**
"Is memory capacity a concern for your AI workloads, or do you prefer expandability over a soldered solution?"
