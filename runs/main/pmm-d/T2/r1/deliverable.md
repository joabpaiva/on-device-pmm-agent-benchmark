# Sales Battlecard: Aurora 14 vs. Vantage Pro 16

## Positioning
Aurora 14 positions as the portable, AI-ready workstation for professionals who need upgradability and future-proof service. It trades raw screen size and GPU VRAM for superior AI performance, portability, and long-term value.

## Aurora 14 Strengths
1. **Upgradable Memory**: Supports up to 128 GB with user-upgradeable slots. Vantage Pro 16 is limited to 64 GB soldered RAM.
2. **AI Performance**: Features a 60 TOPS NPU vs. 40 TOPS, providing greater headroom for running large local AI models.
3. **Service**: Includes a 3-year on-site warranty, compared to Vantage's 1-year depot service.

## Vantage Pro 16 Strengths
1. **Screen**: Larger 16-inch display with a 165 Hz refresh rate, preferred for visualization tasks.
2. **GPU VRAM**: Offers 24 GB of GPU memory vs. 16 GB, beneficial for large 3D scenes.

## Objection Handling

**Objection 1: "Aurora is $300 more expensive."**
Response: The higher starting price reflects a premium 60 TOPS NPU for local AI, a 3-year on-site warranty, and user-upgradeable RAM. Vantage's lower price locks you into soldered 64 GB RAM.

**Objection 2: "I need 24 GB of VRAM for my 3D work."**
Response: Vantage Pro 16 does offer 24 GB VRAM, which is superior for large GPU-bound scenes. However, Aurora's upgradable memory and higher NPU throughput provide better long-term value for AI workflows.

**Objection 3: "The 16-inch screen on the Vantage is better for my work."**
Response: The Vantage's 16-inch IPS screen is indeed larger with a 165 Hz refresh rate. Aurora compensates with a lighter 1.65 kg chassis and up to 11 hours of battery life, making it easier to carry.

## Discovery Question
"When you consider your workflow, do you prioritize immediate screen real estate, or do you need the ability to upgrade your memory later as your software demands grow?"
