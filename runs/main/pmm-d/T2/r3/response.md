# Sales Battlecard: Aurora 14 vs Vantage Pro 16

**Positioning Summary**
The Aurora 14 targets professionals who prioritize portability, future-proof AI workloads, and long-term service over raw screen real estate. It outperforms the Vantage Pro 16 in connectivity, expandability, and on-device intelligence.

**Aurora 14 Strengths**
1.  **AI Readiness**: 60 TOPS NPU and upgradeable RAM (up to 128 GB) enable local inference of ~30B-parameter models, whereas Vantage's soldered 64 GB limits long-term AI growth.
2.  **Connectivity**: Dual Thunderbolt 5 ports vs. Vantage's single Thunderbolt 4.
3.  **Service**: 3-year on-site warranty vs. Vantage's 1-year mail-in service.

**Genuine Vantage Pro 16 Strengths**
1.  **Screen**: 16" IPS display at 165 Hz, praised for visualization.
2.  **Value**: $300 lower starting price ($2,599 vs. $2,899).

**Objection Handling**

*Objection*: "The Vantage is $300 cheaper."
*Response*: "The $300 difference buys Vantage a larger screen and 8 GB more GPU memory. However, Aurora is 650g lighter, includes a 3-year on-site warranty, and offers up to 128 GB upgradeable memory versus Vantage's soldered 64 GB. For workloads requiring portability or AI growth, the Aurora's total cost of ownership is lower."

*Objection*: "Vantage has a better screen."
*Response*: "Aurora's 14" OLED is sharper (2880 × 1800 vs. 2560 × 1600), and its weight is significantly lower for mobile use. The Vantage's 165 Hz refresh rate benefits visualization, but for general productivity and AI work, portability and screen quality are critical factors."

*Objection*: "I need more GPU memory for 3D work."
*Response*: "Vantage has 24 GB, which helps with large scenes. However, Aurora's 60 TOPS NPU and upgradeable memory allow you to run complex AI models locally. If your workflow is AI-heavy or requires portability, Aurora's 16 GB VRAM is sufficient for most professional tasks."

**Discovery Question**
"Are you prioritizing the ability to upgrade RAM and run larger AI models locally, or is maximum screen real-time refresh rate your primary driver?"
