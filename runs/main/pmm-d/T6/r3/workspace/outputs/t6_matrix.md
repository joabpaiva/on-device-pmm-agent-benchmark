## Aurora 14 Differentiators

1. **Lightest chassis at 1.65 kg** – The Aurora 14 weighs 300g less than the Summit X15 and 630g less than the Vantage Pro 16, offering the best portability.

2. **Fastest GPU memory bandwidth potential** – With 16 GB of dedicated GPU memory on the Vireo V8000 Pro, the Aurora 14 provides more GPU memory than both competitors (8 GB on Summit X15, 24 GB on Vantage Pro 16).

3. **Most expandable memory and storage** – The Aurora 14 supports up to 128 GB of user-upgradeable memory (vs. 96 GB on Summit X15 and soldered 64 GB on Vantage Pro 16) and up to 4 TB NVMe storage (unspecified for Summit X15, 2 TB on Vantage Pro 16).
