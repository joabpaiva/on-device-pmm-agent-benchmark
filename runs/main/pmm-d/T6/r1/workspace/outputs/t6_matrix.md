# Workstation Comparison

| Attribute | Aurora 14 | Summit X15 | Vantage Pro 16 |
|-----------|-----------|------------|----------------|
| CPU | Helix H9 AI, 16 cores | Helix H7 AI, 12 cores | Corvex X12, 14 cores |
| GPU | Vireo V8000 Pro, 16 GB dedicated memory | Vireo V6000, 8 GB | Vireo V9000, 24 GB |
| Max Memory | 128 GB (user-upgradeable) | 96 GB | 64 GB (soldered) |
| Max Storage | 4 TB NVMe | not stated | 2 TB |
| Display | 14-inch OLED, 2880 x 1800, 120 Hz | 15.6-inch OLED, 3840 x 2400, 60 Hz | 16-inch IPS, 2560 x 1600, 165 Hz |
| Weight | 1.65 kg | 1.95 kg | 2.30 kg |
| Battery | 86 Wh | not stated | 99 Wh |
| Ports | 2 x Thunderbolt 5, 1 x USB-A, HDMI 2.1, SD card reader | 2 x Thunderbolt 4, 1 x USB-A, HDMI 2.1 | not stated |

## Aurora 14 Differentiators

1. **Best-in-class port speed and port variety** — Thunderbolt 5 ports (faster than the Summit X15's Thunderbolt 4) plus an SD card reader.

2. **Lightest chassis** — 1.65 kg is 0.30 kg lighter than the Summit X15 and 0.65 kg lighter than the Vantage Pro 16.

3. **Highest memory capacity with user-upgradeability** — 128 GB max with user-upgradeable memory, compared to the Summit X15's 96 GB and the Vantage Pro 16's 64 GB soldered.
