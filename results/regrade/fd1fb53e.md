**Q1. If I buy the base model with 32 GB of memory, can I upgrade to 128 GB myself later?**
Yes. You can upgrade memory yourself. The Aurora 14 has two SO-DIMM slots and supports up to 128 GB. Upgrading does not void the warranty when you use qualified modules from the Aurora compatibility list.
*Source: Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades*

**Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?**
The standard warranty is 3 years (on-site, next-business-day). You can extend it to 4 or 5 years at purchase or within 90 days of purchase.
*Source: Section 1: Warranty and Support*

**Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?**
With a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, you can run up to three external 4K displays or two external displays at 8K (60 Hz). If you use the HDMI 2.1 port in addition to the dock, you can add one more display at 4K (120 Hz).
*Source: Section 3: Docking and External Displays*

**Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?**
If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the self-healing BIOS automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot.
*Source: Section 4: Security Features*
