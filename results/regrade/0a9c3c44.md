**Q1. If I buy the base model with 32 GB of memory, can I upgrade to 128 GB myself later?**
Yes. The Aurora 14 supports up to 128 GB, and customers may upgrade memory themselves. Upgrading does not void the warranty when using qualified modules from the Aurora compatibility list.
*Citation: Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades*

**Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?**
The Aurora 14 includes a 3-year on-site, next-business-day warranty. You can extend coverage to 4 or 5 years at purchase or within 90 days of purchase.
*Citation: Section 1: Warranty and Support*

**Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?**
Through a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, the Aurora 14 supports up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz.
*Citation: Section 3: Docking and External Displays*

**Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?**
The system's self-healing BIOS continuously checks firmware integrity. If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot.
*Citation: Section 4: Security Features*

**Q5. Is Aurora 14 certified for Ubuntu 24.04?**
Not covered in the provided documentation. You should contact your sales representative or the technical support team to confirm OS certification details.
