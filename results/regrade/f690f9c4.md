**Q1. If I buy the base model with 32 GB of memory, can I upgrade to 128 GB myself later?**
Yes, you can upgrade the memory yourself. The Aurora 14 supports up to 128 GB and allows customer upgrades without voiding the warranty, provided you use qualified modules from the Aurora compatibility list.
*Source: Section 2: Memory and Storage Upgrades*

---

**Q2. What warranty comes standard, and can I extend it?**
The standard warranty is 3 years of on-site, next-business-day coverage. You can extend this to 4 or 5 years at the time of purchase or within 90 days of purchase. Accidental damage protection is available as a separate purchase.
*Source: Section 1: Warranty and Support*

---

**Q3. How many external monitors can I run from one dock?**
Through a single Thunderbolt 5 dock, you can run up to three external 4K displays at 60 Hz, or two external displays at up to 8K 60 Hz.
*Source: Section 3: Docking and External Displays*

---

**Q4. What happens if the BIOS is attacked?**
If the BIOS is corrupted or attacked, the system's self-healing BIOS automatically restores a protected copy at the next boot.
*Source: Section 4: Security Features*

---

**Q5. Is Aurora 14 certified for Ubuntu 24.04?**
Not covered in the provided documentation. Please contact your sales representative or check the official Aurora support website for operating system certification details.
