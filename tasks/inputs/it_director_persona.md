# Buyer Persona: IT Director, Mid-Size Engineering Firm

*Fictional persona created for benchmark testing.*

**Name:** Morgan Lee
**Role:** IT Director
**Company:** A 400-person civil engineering firm with three offices

## Situation
- The IT team is cloud-first. Engineers use cloud-hosted virtual workstations for CAD and simulation work.
- The hardware and software budget cycle closes at the end of Q1. Anything not approved by then waits a year.
- Engineers increasingly want to use AI tools on client drawings and project files.

## What Morgan cares about
- Total cost across the fleet, including the monthly cloud workstation bill.
- Security and keeping client project data under the firm's control.
- Fleet manageability: fewer device types, easy upgrades, predictable service.
- Engineer productivity on site visits, where connectivity is unreliable.

## Likely objections
- "We already pay for cloud workstations. Why buy powerful laptops too?"
- "Laptops are harder to manage and secure than cloud desktops."
- "Our budget is locked until the end of Q1."

## Preferred communication
- Short, direct emails. No hype. Facts over adjectives.
